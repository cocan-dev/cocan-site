Privacy

Last updated: 12 November 2026. This page explains what personal data the websites cocan.dev and kirpitch.dev handle, and what happens when you write to us. The short version: we do not track you. We collect nothing from visiting, and we keep your emails only as long as we need them.

Who is responsible

cocan.dev is run by Niky Cocan, Anna and Alex. It is not a registered company yet, so the three of us are responsible for the data described here. For any privacy question, write to hello@cocan.dev. We answer within seven days.

Visiting the websites

  • The sites use no cookies, no analytics, no tracking pixels, no advertising, no embedded videos or maps, no external fonts and no other third-party scripts. Your browser requests every file from the site's own address.
  • The only thing stored is your light or dark theme choice, if you click the theme button. It stays in your browser's local storage on your own device (key theme), is never sent to us or anyone else, and you can clear it in your browser settings. It is needed only to remember a setting you asked for.
  • We do not see who visits, and we do not collect, store or analyse visitor logs or IP addresses.

Our hosting providers

As with any website, the company that serves the pages receives technical data when your browser asks for them, such as your IP address, the time, the page requested and your browser type. We do not receive or use that data. The providers involved are:

  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., a Microsoft company, United States) serves the pages. It handles this data under its own privacy statement, including the safeguards it uses for data sent outside the EU.
  • Hostinger (Lithuania) registers the domain names and answers the DNS lookups (the step that turns the address into a server) for both domains.

The Open source section of cocan.dev shows public information about public repositories (names, descriptions, commit counts and dates). It is prepared in advance and contains no visitor data.

When you write to us

  • What we receive: the email you send us, with your email address, your name if you give it, and whatever you choose to write or attach. Please send only what is needed.
  • Why: to answer you and, if you ask for work, to prepare a proposal or contract. Legal basis: steps taken at your request before a contract, and our legitimate interest in answering messages and in keeping records to defend against legal claims (GDPR Article 6(1)(b) and (f)). You can object to this at any time, see Your rights.
  • Where it is kept: in our mailboxes at Tuta (Germany), an encrypted email service. Tuta handles it under its own privacy policy. Only members of our team can read it.
  • How long: we keep an email conversation for up to three years after the last message, which is the usual period in which a legal claim can still be brought, so that we can answer questions and defend ourselves if a dispute arises. After that we delete it. If we work together, we keep contracts, invoices and accounting records for as long as the law requires, which can be longer.
  • Who gets it: nobody else. We do not sell or share your data, and we do not use it for newsletters or marketing unless you ask for one. We disclose data only when a law or a court order requires it.
  • Security reports: an email to security@cocan.dev, or a private report on GitHub, is handled the same way. Reports on GitHub are also subject to GitHub's privacy statement.

Our projects on GitHub

Our open-source code lives on GitHub. If you open an issue, comment or contribute there, what you write, your GitHub username and any email address in your commits are public by design and are handled by GitHub under its own terms. Please do not post anything there that you want to keep private.

The Kirpitch software

Kirpitch, the desktop program, runs on your own computer and has no telemetry. Nothing leaves your machine unless you choose a cloud language-model provider yourself. In that case your data go to that provider under your own agreement with it, not through us.

Your rights

You may ask us to tell you what data we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict how we use it, or to give you a copy. You may object to our use of your data. Write to hello@cocan.dev. We do not make decisions about you by automated means. You also have the right to complain to your national data protection authority.

Children

These websites are for businesses and developers. They are not aimed at children, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change what we do, we update this page and the date above. If we add anything that stores data or contacts other servers when you visit, we will say so here first.

What we do

  • Automation

    The work that repeats, done by software. Filing, chasing, re-typing, sorting the inbox. We use plain scripts and workflows, and language-model agents (the chat-style AI) where they actually help. Ordinary code does the rest, so you can check what it did.

    Invoices that file themselves. An inbox that sorts itself. Reports that write themselves.

  • Hardware and firmware

    Complete systems, designed as one: the board and the code that runs on it. From schematic to a prototype you can hold. We take this work when it comes to us or when it pairs with automation.

How we work

  1. 01

    You write to us

    Tell us about your idea. We reply within three working days.

  2. 02

    A free call

    One call, about 30 to 45 minutes, so we understand what you want.

  3. 03

    A written proposal

    Scope, timeline and price, in writing. If the idea has real unknowns, we may suggest a short paid feasibility study first.

  4. 04

    We build

    In short steps, with something you can try every week or two.

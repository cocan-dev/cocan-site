What we do

  • MVPs

    The smallest version of your product, software or hardware, that is worth shipping, built so you can learn from real users quickly.

  • Prototypes

    Working proofs of concept, in software or hardware, to test an idea, a technology or a workflow before committing to it.

  • AI-powered tools

    Practical tools that use large language models where they help, with deterministic code doing the rest.

How we work

  1. 01

    You write to us

    Tell us about your idea. We reply within three working days.

  2. 02

    Free scoping call

    One call, about 30 to 45 minutes, to understand what you want to build.

  3. 03

    Written proposal

    Scope, timeline and price in writing. For uncertain ideas we may suggest a short, paid feasibility study first.

  4. 04

    We build

    In short iterations, with something you can try every week or two.
